About

Ninjago

Ninjago follows the journey of six ninja warriors — Lloyd, Kai, Jay, Cole, Zane, and Nya — as they protect the land of Ninjago from powerful villains. Trained by Master Wu, the ninja use their elemental abilities and Spinjitzu skills to face threats like the Overlord, Garmadon, and many other enemies. Throughout their adventures, they discover new realms, build strong friendships, and learn the true meaning of being a ninja. Together, they continue to defend Ninjago and maintain balance across the world.

Meet

The Team

The Ninja Team is united by trust, courage, and unwavering teamwork, standing together against every threat that faces Ninjago. Each ninja brings unique powers and skills, but their greatest strength comes from working as one. Through friendship, loyalty, and determination, they overcome impossible challenges and protect the realm from evil. Together, Lloyd, Kai, Jay, Cole, Zane, and Nya prove that true heroes are strongest when they fight side by side.
