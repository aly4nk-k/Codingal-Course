Ninja Verse

Meet The Characters of Ninjago

|

|

Garmadon

Garmadon is the son of First Spinjitzu Master and the older brother of Wu, once corrupted by the Great Devourer’s venom.

The Overlord

The ancient embodiment of darkness and the sworn enemy of the First Spinjitzu Master and his descendants.

Samukai

A four-armed skeleton warlord who served Garmadon and once ruled the Underworld.

Pythor

The last known Anacondrai and a cunning leader who sought to revive the Great Devourer.

Skales

A powerful Hypnobrai general who became the leader of the Serpentine after Pythor's defeat.

Master Chen

A manipulative warlord who sought to conquer Ninjago using the power of Anacondrai.

Clouse

Master Chen's right-hand man and a powerful practitioner of dark magic.

Morro

A former student of Wu who became the master of wind and sought to fulfill a dark prophecy.

Nadakhan

A Djinn pirate who used magical wishes to manipulate others and restore his kingdom of Djinjago.

Krux

An Elemental Master of Time who betrayed his fellow masters and plotted to change history.

Acronix

An Elemental Master of Time who joined his brother Krux in their plan to conquer Ninjago.

General Cryptor

A Nindroid general who served the Overlord and commanded the Nindroid army.

Mister E

A mysterious Nindroid and member of the Sons of Garmadon who hunted the ninja.

Harumi

The Quiet One and adopted daughter of the Emperor and Empress of Ninjago City.

Killow

A powerful member of the Sons of Garmadon and the leader of the Street Rats.

Ultra Violet

A reckless and dangerous member of the Sons of Garmadon known for her fearless attitude.

Iron Baron

A ruthless dragon hunter who ruled the Dragon Hunters in the First Realm.

The Omega

The leader of the Oni who sought to cover Ninjago and the realms in darkness.

Aspheera

A powerful Serpentine sorceress who mastered forbidden fire and sought revenge against Wu.

Unagami

The artificial intelligence behind Prime Empire who sought to become a real living being.

Mechanic

The Mechanic is a skilled criminal and inventor who uses advanced technology to carry out his schemes.

Skull Sorcerer

A mysterious sorcerer who used the Shadow of the Skull to control the creatures beneath Shintaro.

Prince Kalmaar

The arrogant prince of Merlopia who plotted against his father and sought to control Wojira.

Empress Beatrix

The ruthless Empress of Imperium who used dragon energy to maintain control over her realm.

Rapton

A dragon hunter who served Lord Ras and helped capture dragons for Imperium.

Lord Ras

A tiger-like warrior who manipulates others while pursuing his mysterious plans across the realms.

Jordana

A skilled warrior who became involved with Lord Ras and his plans against the ninja.

Forbidden Five

A group of ancient and powerful warriors who seek to conquer the realms.

Zilvar

Major villain in season 4 part 2 trying to save his tribe.

Dr Larow

Dr. LaRow is a evil scientist who follows empress beatrix.
